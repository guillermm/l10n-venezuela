ISLR Sale and Purchase Functionalities
======================================

Default ISLR concept on the product is copied to sale and purchase order
lines, then onto the generated invoice lines so withholding can be applied
at payment time.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

No extra configuration. Requires ``l10n_ve_withholding_islr``.

Usage
=====

Select the ISLR concept on quotation or purchase lines. It is copied to
the invoice; the withholding itself is applied in Register Payment.
