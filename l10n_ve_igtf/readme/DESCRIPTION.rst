Venezuela IGTF
==============

IGTF on payments through foreign-currency or crypto journals (Odoo 17).

- Flag per journal; rate and accounts on the company.
- Applied on the payment and on Register Payment.
- Optional IGTF on vendor bills: extra expense line that increases the
  residual. Register Payment does not auto-apply IGTF if the bill already
  has it (avoids charging twice).
- Squared entry: extra IGTF on the collection/payment; liquidity line
  uses the outstanding account.

No TXT / SENIAT declaration in this version. New module (not from the
historical Vauxoo 8.0 tree).
