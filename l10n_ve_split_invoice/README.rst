Split Invoices
==============

When posting a customer invoice or credit note that exceeds the company
line limit, extra product lines are copied to a new draft invoice and
removed from the original. The new document keeps ``split_parent_id``.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Set the maximum number of product lines per invoice on the company
(``lines_invoice``).

Usage
=====

Post a customer invoice with more product lines than the company limit.
A second draft invoice is created with the overflow lines.
