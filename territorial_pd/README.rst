Venezuelan Municipalities and Parishes
======================================

Political division of Venezuela (states, municipalities and parishes)
integrated with ``res.country`` and ``res.country.state``.

**Source.** Models and CSV catalog were taken from
``territorial_pd`` 16.0 of
`odoo-mastercore/odoo-venezuela <https://github.com/odoo-mastercore/odoo-venezuela/tree/16.0/territorial_pd>`_,
authors **SINAPSYS GLOBAL SA** and **MASTERCORE SAS**
(Reydi Hernández, Freddy Arraez, Elvis Paez). Ported to Odoo 17 by
Guillermo Montoya. Partner address fields (municipality / parish) follow
``l10n_ve_base`` of the same repository.

Official Odoo 17 does not ship Venezuelan states. This module loads the
24 states, municipalities and parishes. Nationality (gentilicio) is
added on ``res.country``.

Configuration
=============

1. Install this module **before** ``l10n_ve_fiscal_requirements`` (it is
   a dependency of that module).
2. Contacts > Configuration > Localization: **Municipalities** and
   **Parishes**.
3. On a Venezuelan partner, choose State, then Municipality, then Parish.
   The free-text City field remains available for the locality name.

Municipal withholding (``l10n_ve_withholding_muni``) uses these
municipalities for ordinance rates.

Usage
=====

On the partner form, when the country is Venezuela, Municipality and
Parish appear under State. Changing the state clears a municipality that
does not belong to it.
