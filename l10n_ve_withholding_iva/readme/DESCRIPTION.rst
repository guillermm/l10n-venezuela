Management Withholding VAT Venezuelan Laws
===========================================

VAT withholding (Retención de IVA) for Odoo 17, SENIAT rules:

- Rate on the partner (75 % standard or 100 % special taxpayer).
- Applied when registering a payment (write-off); the invoice residual drops.
- Breakdown by aliquot and exempt base; official QWeb voucher.
- TXT 99035 in VES (fortnight, latin-1) for the SENIAT portal.
