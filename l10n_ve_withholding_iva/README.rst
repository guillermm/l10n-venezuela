Management Withholding VAT Venezuelan Laws
===========================================

VAT withholding (Retención de IVA) for Odoo 17, SENIAT rules:

- Rate on the partner (75 % standard or 100 % special taxpayer).
- Applied when registering a payment (write-off); the invoice residual drops.
- Breakdown by aliquot and exempt base; official QWeb voucher.
- TXT 99035 in VES (fortnight, latin-1) for the SENIAT portal.

Configuration
=============

On the company, set the default VAT withholding journal and account
(7-digit codes). On each supplier, set the IVA withholding rate.

Usage
=====

1. Post the vendor bill (no voucher is created yet).
2. Accounting > Vendors > Register Payment: review *IVA a Retener*.
3. Confirm the payment; the voucher and write-off are created together.
4. Review vouchers under Accounting > Vendors > Vendor VAT Withholdings.
5. Customer withholdings received: enter the amount and voucher number on
   the inbound payment wizard.
6. Accounting > Reporting > Generate TXT SENIAT (IVA).
